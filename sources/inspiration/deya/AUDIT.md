interesting... I still want us to be able to have our own identity with the slides that's different from Deya's".

All 22 videos in this folder, read from their distinct-frame boards (YouTube storyboard frames, 320x180, about one
every 10 s, so small text is often unreadable). Each point cites `video id m:ss`. What we take from it, and what
we make our own, is in `rules/slide-method.md` 3f.

## 2. The face

- A big circle with no frame at all, usually 45-60% of the frame's height; sometimes a rounded rectangle or an
  arch (JEbpzybCeZ8, o7blrkYeNo4, WwHeSa0xv4E). It moves round the slide from slide to slide.
- Things touch it: a cat on top of it, a flower behind it (SE401zf_fgM 0:38), a book cover sticker on its corner
  (o7blrkYeNo4), a graduation cap on her head (J24Z91Wk8oQ 4:18), a crown (6EP6Iv4qffs 0:47).
- Between slides she is full frame, talking, often with her real cat walking in (JEbpzybCeZ8 5:22: "oh please don't
  walk over the computer").

## 3. A funny literal cut-out on almost every content slide

Real photos cut out, used as visual puns for the point being made:
- eYym3GXaWfs: "The Traffic Problem" = a traffic light and a line of ducks (3:22); "Conversion" = a crying $100 bill
  (8:17); "Delivery" = a Bentley (9:50); "Energy" = a cat in a burning inbox (11:22); "Clarity" = a grumpy cat by a
  loading spinner (13:22).
- 0lfeGPW3BcY: "passive income is king" = an hourglass (0:19) and the mocking SpongeBob meme (0:29); "winners
  outwork everyone" = a typewriter and The Rock (8:04, 8:24); "find your niche" = a magnifier (5:58).
- TSUJcXNE3t0: the Good Idea Formula sits between the pyramids and a castle (1:07); cats in the bottom corners.
- LLK4ti6AnB0: a screaming cat (1:32); a gold treasure chest for "the Magic solution" (8:26).
- 8TgNtmQ4Vko: black-and-white hands, statues, binoculars, a megaphone, as if cut from an old book.
- The joke recurs: her cat is in almost every video, as a photo, a meme or walking through the shot.

## 4. Her own real things, never generic stock

- Her YouTube channel page (JEbpzybCeZ8 1:57, 5:32, 11:23, 12:22, 14:29: "strategy #1-6", with green tape strips
  and a hand-drawn arrow on it), her Notion system (tEYF6VvgOic 8:36-19:09), her revenue screenshots 2023 vs 2024
  (6EP6Iv4qffs 10:05, 11:11), her comments (JEbpzybCeZ8 5:51), her own notebook and printed plan (6Y_K34Eecy8,
  ekpA_Ya1DCg).
- Book covers of what she read, as stickers (o7blrkYeNo4, 6EP6Iv4qffs, qITpEEGskck).
- When a photo is not hers, it is a meme or a famous face (Alysa Liu, SE401zf_fgM 3:41; Sabrina Carpenter,
  o7blrkYeNo4 8:01; The Rock), never a stock photo of an office.

## 5. Builds and a slide that comes back

- The same slide returns between chapters with one more thing filled in: folders (LLK4ti6AnB0 0:09, 4:45, 6:35,
  7:40), padlocks opening (1D7RsOVC-lE), a ranking board filling (0lfeGPW3BcY), blacked-out boxes revealed
  (8TgNtmQ4Vko, 5cFmOZG-LfU), the bookshelf (qITpEEGskck), a table of contents covered in tape strips, then
  uncovered (SE401zf_fgM 2:14, 7:03).
- Lists grow one item per frame (J24Z91Wk8oQ assignments 2:42-4:09; ZTKe2YT_T7Y notes 3:04-5:10).

## 6. Type

- Short titles, 0-4 lines under them. A serif or condensed serif title, a script word inside it ("Systems",
  "small businesses", LLK4ti6AnB0 0:36, 2:45), mono for the body, often underlined.
- Meme case for a word she mocks: "mEsSy" (qITpEEGskck 7:41), "rAnDoM" (5cFmOZG-LfU 1:38), "pAsSivE" (0lfeGPW3BcY
  0:29). Wrong answers struck through (eYym3GXaWfs 2:36; 8TgNtmQ4Vko 26:32).
- In a themed video the type joins the world: a pixel face for the game, a Windows 95 face for the systems video.

## 7. Grounds and edges

Paper, grid paper, crumpled paper, a dark noise ground, soft pastel glows; torn paper strips at an edge (TSUJcXNE3t0,
8TgNtmQ4Vko, ZTKe2YT_T7Y); objects cropped by the frame edge.

## 8. What she does not do

- No tape stuck on a photo to decorate it. Her tape is a label or a cover: the redaction strips on the table of
  contents (SE401zf_fgM 2:14), the labels on THE MAGIC DOC (JEW5Zv7f4Og 1:08), short green strips pointing at parts
  of her channel page (JEbpzybCeZ8 1:57). Binder clips hold a document up (qITpEEGskck 14:04, 19:28).
- No polaroid frames, no rows of identical cards, no icon grids, no gradient buttons, no office stock photos.
- No full paragraphs on screen.
