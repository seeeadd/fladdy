# After Harvest YouTube slide kit

The pieces for making After Harvest YouTube slides in the light-set look. The guide that explains how to use them is
`Fladdy-Slide-Guide.pdf` (in this kit, and in the repo at `guide/`). Built 2026-10-29 from github.com/seeeadd/fladdy.

## What is in each folder

| folder | what | use |
|---|---|---|
| 0 guide | Fladdy-Slide-Guide.pdf | Jason Fladlien's slide method, our look, the layouts, the process, checklists, the audit |
| 1 fonts | Olivera (demo), Olivera Oblique (demo), Literata + italic, Hanken Grotesk, IBM Plex Mono | headlines, script line, lead-ins, body and shout, labels and sources |
| 2 colours and type | tokens.json, colors.css | the hex codes and type roles |
| 3 props (Canva photo cut-outs) | wheat, straw hat, kraft folder, receipt, graph paper, tickets, envelope, planner, tape | at most two per slide, at the edges. Tape is in the kit but never goes on a photo |
| 4 stitch art | both scarecrows, wheat bundle, rose, flowers, lavender, coin, check, the stitch icons, the "1" | After Harvest's own cross-stitch |
| 5 hand marks | olive circle, olive loop arrow | one mark per slide at most |
| 6 real pictures | the Launch Lab page and ticket, Jay Clouse's post, our chat with Jay, The Lab photos; `stock-*` placeholders | real proof; every file's source is in its SOURCES.md |
| 7 face cam | sean-cam-placeholder.jpg | stands in for the live camera |
| 8 slide references (light set) | the 11 approved slides | the base layouts |

## Type

| voice | font | use |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | Olivera (demo) | thickened with a 0.022em stroke; no punctuation in the demo, a Literata glyph stands in |
| Script | Olivera Oblique (demo) | the script word or line in a headline |
| Lead-in | Literata italic | small line above a headline, sentence case |
| Shout, body | Hanken Grotesk 800-900 | the shout, bold lines |
| Labels | IBM Plex Mono | typewriter lines, labels, source lines |

Sizes at 1920 x 1080: headline 112-176px, key line 56px+, supporting line 40px+, source line 18-20px.

## The rules in one place

1. Ask "How do we show this?" of every line; answer with a real thing.
2. The biggest words carry the belief; never the title, the number or the price.
3. One idea per slide; each part appears as it is said.
4. Two colours per slide: ink and rust. Sage only as a ground.
5. At most two type voices per slide, plus the source line.
6. At most two props, at the edges, each meaning something.
7. Real screenshots with a source line under them; never tape on a photo.
8. The face circle on every slide; nothing covers the key words.

Fonts: Olivera is the free demo (Sean, 2026-10-28: use the demo). Literata, Hanken Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono are
under the SIL Open Font License.
