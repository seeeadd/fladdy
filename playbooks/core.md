## The ideas that run through everything

1. **Show it.** Ask "how do we show this?" of every line and answer with a real thing: a screenshot, a receipt, a
   real photo [Jason slides 37:09, 20:05]. (slides.md)
2. **Hook for buyers, not attention.** Favorable, qualified attention that primes the sale; the pop quiz first
   [doc L11-23, L547-561]. (hooks.md)
3. **Pain before gain, in doses.** Remove their excuses and they buy; enough pain to move them, never hopeless
   [doc L1211-1217, L1341-1595]. (pain.md)
4. **Tease the whole way.** Give the outcome, hide the how; be specific; reveal one line at a time [doc L2029-2047,
   L3127-3169]. (curiosity.md)
5. **Never stop at the feature.** What it is, what it does, how it changes their life; then stack proof [doc
   L3341-3407, L3773]. (excite.md)
6. **Position two things.** You (nobody else can solve this) and the opportunity (why now, why the normal way
   fails), with direct proof shown "in the wild" [doc L4011-4140]. (positioning.md)
7. **The paradigm makes buying the only logical choice.** His favourite: show the manual way in full, sell the
   automatic way [doc L4823-4861]. (paradigm.md)
8. **Build a bridge of small yeses** to the pitch; ask, don't claim [doc L8449-8461, L8687-8697]. (commitment.md)
9. **Close:** "thus far" yeses, you can do it alone, or another option together, take on responsibility, "would
   you love to hear more?" [doc L9081-9201]. (close.md)
10. **The offer slide is the most important slide**; the price must feel like a deal, anchored high to low with a
    reason why [doc L11151, L12507]. (offer.md, price.md)
11. **Bonuses close the sale; saying no must feel riskier than yes; scarcity only when it is real** [doc L14563,
    L16631, L16919]. (bonuses.md, risk.md, scarcity.md)
12. **On YouTube, teaching is the sale.** One unlisted core video, every other video points to it, soft sell
    throughout, no invented scarcity [Jason new way 1:48; Jason new webinar 12:31-13:13]. (youtube.md)
13. **Know the numbers.** Every funnel step has a benchmark; fix the one easiest to double [Jeremy 1:30:20,
    1:31:41]. (funnel-numbers.md)

## Sean's standing rulings (2026-10-28; full text in `rulings.md`)

- Type: Olivera headlines, Literata subheadings, Hanken Grotesk body; the Olivera demo is fine.
- YouTube slides: simple, adaptable foundations, not overly After Harvest.
- Not "too much like Claude": no templated stacks, real artefacts, human touch. Not messy either: nicely made.
- The YouTube looks so far: the dark stage, a layout library, the light set (the base, 2026-10-29), the sewn set
  (rejected: "a very bad recreation of Deya's"). Slides are made in HTML ("you design better using HTML"); real
  pictures and screenshots, never tape stuck on a photo (rules/slide-method.md 3d-3f).
