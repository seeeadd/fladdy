# Bonuses playbook

Read this when: designing or presenting bonuses (choosing, naming, the bonus slides, the value stack, the webinar-only special).
Sources: `sources/fladlien/notes/full-methodology.md` L14563-16616 (Bonuses Masterclass, step 12, with its After Harvest Black Friday application). Built 2026-10-29. The source is the master: re-check a quote there.

## In 5 lines
- Bonuses close the sale: give each one 2-4 slides, more than any core module gets.
- Aim for 7, each a different kind (training, tool, templates, case study, community, physical, service), each built on proof, each removing one objection.
- Give every bonus a cool name and a dollar value, and mention it in the teaching before you reveal it.
- After the final pitch, before objections: one webinar-only special, negotiated, qualified, timestamped, never mentioned again.
- Every bonus carries real scarcity; a bonus that does not pull its weight leaves the pitch (but is still delivered).

## The rules

**Why bonuses**
1. Bonuses are step 12, the "most important step in my opinion in the webinar process". A webinar that does well but could do better should fix its bonuses first. [doc L14571-14575]
2. The core offer gets interest; bonuses close. If they don't make someone think "I'd buy it just for the bonuses", they are not strong enough. [doc L16461-16465, L16591-16593]
3. When the content already ties everything to the offer, mention the course in one line and spend the time on bonuses (his affiliate offers). [doc L14577-14585, L15133-15139]
4. Carrot and stick: regular price with no bonuses ($997), webinar price lower with all of them ($497). More for less "short circuits the mind". [doc L14587-14595]

**Best practices**
5. More time on bonuses than on the core: 1 slide per core module, 2-4 slides per bonus, sometimes 3-4 times as long. [doc L14599-14617]
6. Your best stuff goes in as free bonuses: cannot be bought, and the word "free". [doc L14619-14631, L16467-16471]
7. No proof, no bonus: client interviews, case studies, live demonstrations, results. [doc L14633-14645]
8. Bonuses audition: swap out one that does not hold its weight, keep delivering it, stop pitching it. [doc L14647-14667, L16511-16515]
9. Mix modalities. Training on top of training overwhelms; training + tool + templates + service is "irresistible". [doc L14669-14683, L16479-16483]
10. Bonuses can cost sales: too many (overwhelm), wrong ones (new objections), one modality (redundancy), no scarcity (no urgency). A bonus must remove an objection or add value, or be cut. [doc L14685-14695, L16505-16509]

**The kinds**
11. Training. Interviews are the easiest and built on proof (two hours of questions is a bonus). Recorded training needs a cool name, an "every secret" promise, the areas covered, a proof number. A coming live call needs nothing built before you sell, adds a date, and gets recorded. [doc L14707-14777]
12. Ebooks "essentially suck" as bonuses ($7-10 on Kindle) unless tied to a proof element. [doc L14779-14793]
13. Software: show manual, sell automatic. Teach the tedious steps, then show the tool doing them in two clicks. [doc L14795-14835]
14. Community can feel like homework ("Now I gotta show up?"): make it optional, time-limited, specific, with a guaranteed response (TWF Prime: 90 days, monthly live Q&A, an answer to every post). [doc L14837-14879]
15. Physical items must simplify their life: books worked; travel, tasks and logistics did not. [doc L14881-14901]
16. Templates, swipes, done-for-you: usable at once, no blank page. [doc L14903-14927]
17. Services don't scale: use them as high-end bonuses or for small beta groups. [doc L14929-14945]
18. Case study breakdowns: every element of a success, what worked and why, made replicable. [doc L14947-14961]

**Presenting**
19. Name each like a product: memorable, suggests benefit or speed, easy to say ("The [Cool Name] System", never "Bonus training on X"). [doc L14965-14985]
20. Foreshadow: mention the person or tool 2-3 times in the content, then reveal it as a bonus. [doc L14987-15009]
21. Stack visually: numbered, a dollar value each, what it is and why it matters, total value against the investment. [doc L15011-15045]
22. Proof is specific: numbers, real names, results, before and after, timestamps. [doc L15067-15083]
23. Scarcity in every bonus: webinar-only, a real limited quantity, a real deadline, or a qualification ("For people who stayed through whole webinar"). Say webinar-only, then remove it after. [doc L15085-15119, L15571-15591, L16491-16495]
24. Seven bonuses: push to 9 if needed, never 10+. 4-5 numbered plus 2-3 unnumbered extras (fast-action, webinar-only), 14-28 slides. [doc L15141-15191]

**The webinar-only special** ("The greatest technique I've ever done" [doc L15197])
25. Live webinar only, never mentioned again after its slide, never on the sales page. It lifts conversion, brings people to the next webinar and builds trust when you keep your word. [doc L15199-15209, L15521-15535]
26. Order: regular bonuses, final pitch, the special, objection handling, Q&A, final close. [doc L15405-15429]
27. Five forms: a new item; an existing bonus doubled or more; longer access (to lifetime); more of it (1 call to 3); a better service (group to one-on-one). [doc L15361-15403]
28. Cut only its slides from the replay; keep all objection handling. [doc L15457-15465]
29. Keep the end live: ask who is signing up, name them, help with browser trouble. Others see someone buying. [doc L15473-15519]
30. Practice it at a mirror: the pause, reveal, qualification, ending, timing. "The pause IS part of the technique." [doc L15537-15569]

## How to do it

**Audit each bonus** [doc L16391-16457]: a cool name that suggests the benefit; a believable dollar value above what they pay; a format no other bonus uses; proof; a real reason for its scarcity; one objection removed; foreshadowed earlier; 2-4 clear slides.

**One bonus, 2-4 slides** [doc L15051-15065]
- BONUS #[n]: [Cool Name] (Value: $[x]) + its promise
- What's included: [breakdown]
- Proof: [name] got [result] in [time]
- Why you can't get it anywhere else: [exclusivity]

**The stack**: BONUS #1-#7 "[Name] (Value: $[x])", TOTAL VALUE $[sum], YOUR INVESTMENT $[price] [doc L15013-15035]. Build it live, one bonus at a time, pointing: "Plus you're getting..." [doc L16355-16369]

**30-second pitch per bonus**: name, what it is, why it matters, proof or hook, one sentence each. [doc L16321-16333]

**Foreshadow, then reveal**: "one of the best examples I've seen is [Name] who made $[X] using this exact approach", later "Remember [Name] who I mentioned earlier? ... I actually sat down and interviewed them for 2 hours ... And that interview is Bonus #1." [doc L16343-16353]

**The webinar-only special, in order** [doc L15221-15359, L15439-15471]
1. Pause on the slide (about 3 seconds, on purpose): "You may wonder why I paused just here. It's because what I'm about to say next will be forever deleted from the annals of webinar history."
2. Permission: "I had to negotiate with [partner] to even be able to do this... We will only put a strict number on it. Now I'm not gonna tell you what that number is, but if we hit that number on this webinar, I will let you know."
3. Qualification: "If you've shown up on this webinar and you stayed this whole time ... it's you ... ONLY if you're here on the webinar."
4. Timestamp: "If you're even a second after the webinar, you do not get this bonus."
5. Invisibility: "When you go to [URL], you will see hide nor hair mentioned of this bonus on this page ... Trust us, it will show up to you."
6. Stack: add an item, then upgrade it ("lifetime access, not just one year").
7. Final warning: last chance, invite questions.
8. Chop point: answer questions on this bonus only, then "you will still get this bonus as long as it's done before I end this webinar". Go to objections. Hint early you "could just chop off the end"; at the end, "if there's anybody signing up right now, let me know."

## Mistakes he calls out
- "Let me give you more training" as the bonus for a training product. [doc L14675-14681]
- A bonus that is an objection: "Come to Canton Fair in China with us". [doc L14697-14703]
- The special done unpracticed: "The first few times I see people do it, it's not good." [doc L15539-15541]
- Fake scarcity: "If you say 'limited spots,' mean it." [doc L16493]
- "Don't just throw in some PDFs and call it a day." [doc L16587]
