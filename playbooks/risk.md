# Risk playbook

Read this when: writing guarantees, risk reversal, handling objections (the guarantee slide, the objection section after the offer, the closes, a sales call).
Sources: `sources/fladlien/notes/full-methodology.md` L16617-16918 (Guarantee and Objection Handling Masterclass). Built 2026-10-29. The source is the master: re-check a quote there.

## In 5 lines
- Show that saying no is riskier than saying yes: sell the cost of inaction.
- Pick the guarantee on purpose: unconditional, conditional (skin in the game) or better than money back (his favourite).
- A double-your-money-back with an easy daily condition removes the risk, and almost nobody claims it.
- Sort each objection into one of six kinds and answer it; testimonials are the antidote to most risks.
- Spend hours on objections: "That's where all the money is."

## The rules

**The frame**
1. Risk has two tools: guarantees (remove the risk) and objections (handle the belief). [doc L16625-16629]
2. Make it riskier to say no than yes: show the cost of inaction, not only the benefit of action. [doc L16623, L16875-16877]
3. Be willing to spend hours handling objections. [doc L16623, L16867-16869]

**Guarantees**
4. Unconditional: 30 days, no questions. "Every penny" makes the investment feel small, "for any reason or none at all" removes the need to justify, "the opportunity to gain" turns risk into opportunity. [doc L16633-16645]
5. Triple guarantee, to "look even cooler": best support; you'll never have to [pain]; money back in 30 days, no reason needed. [doc L16647-16655]
6. Conditional: when you need skin in the game, or when inflation is high, because refund rates rise with it. Do the math: conversion gained against refunds paid. [doc L16657-16659, L16859-16861]
7. Sell a conditional as "for fast action takers only", with the business reason the deal needs it, and the boogeyman close: "I'm trying to TRICK YOU into your own success." [doc L16661-16667]
8. Better than money back is his favourite ("These are my jam"): the standard 30 days, OR double your money back in 60 days if they follow your method and don't double their investment. [doc L16669-16675]
9. Make the double-money condition easy to picture and rarely done: a shared Google Doc, 60 days of a morning goal and an evening report, two paragraphs a day. "You're lucky if you get 1% compliance on that." Winners do it, so the condition pushes people to act like winners. [doc L16677-16699, L16863-16865]
10. Name the guarantee and layer more than one protection. [doc L16909, L16917]

**Objections**
11. Every objection is one of six: money, time, confidence or competence, alternatives, procrastination, product-specific. [doc L16707-16721]
12. Move them up the ladder with each piece of evidence: no one can do it; you can, but that's it; you and some others; you and most others; "Okay, I'll try to do it". [doc L16723-16737]
13. Testimonials are the antidote to most risks: stack different backgrounds, starting points, speeds of success and levels of commitment. [doc L16739-16751]
14. Use confusion to unstick a belief held without thinking, and childhood references. [doc L16767, L16779, L16871-16873, L16907]
15. Remove "do nothing" as an option. [doc L16795]
16. Close in the intro, close in the content, close in the close; treat every objection as a gift. [doc L16891-16893]

## How to do it

**Unconditional** [doc L16637]: "When you sign up you're also protected by a 30 day no questions asked money back guarantee. If you're not satisfied, neither am I. For any reason or none at all, just contact us let us know. We'll return every penny of your investment. You're 100% protected. You only have the opportunity to gain when you sign up."

**Conditional** [doc L16663]: "WARNING: This course and bonuses are reserved for fast action takers only. Only sign up if you plan on [first result] within [30 days]. [Why the deal needs it.] That's why we have a conditional guarantee. If you sign up and don't do just the bare minimum, you do not get to ask for your money back. However, if you just [bare minimum] in [30 days] and you're not over the moon excited, we'll cheerfully give you your full investment back."

**Double your money back** [doc L16675, L16703]: "Your investment is twice protected. We got your standard guarantee - for any reason or none at all, if you want your money back, you got it. 30 days. OR your double money back guarantee - just follow one of my [product] secrets and within 60 days, if you don't make double your investment, I'll give you DOUBLE your money back." Then: "It costs nothing. It's so simple... if you're the unluckiest SOB in the world, I'll still compensate you for your time."

**The closes**
- Does it still work? "Does breathing still work? ... The tactics might evolve, but the strategy? That's timeless." [doc L16759]
- I'm scared (his favourite): "Are you afraid you're gonna fail? Or are you afraid you're gonna succeed? ... you KNOW failure ... But success? You have NO CLUE what that's like." [doc L16765]
- Passenger seat: "we're gonna put fear in the passenger seat ... But fear no longer gets to DRIVE the vehicle. Can you do that for me?" [doc L16771]
- Ride the bike: "the first time's the hardest ... Thank God that younger you didn't give up ... Even with scraped elbows, even with bruised knees." It talks to them as a child. [doc L16773-16779]
- Three options: "Do what you've always done", "Give up and do something completely different", "Double down with our additional advantages, resources, community, and support". "Everybody wants to double down." [doc L16781-16795]
- Fence: "Sitting on the fence is uncomfortable, and if you do it too long, it leads to hemorrhoids." [doc L16801]
- Two pains: "The pain of commitment weighs ounces. The pain of regret weighs tons." [doc L16805]

**Checklist** [doc L16895-16917]: the guarantee that fits; their top 3 objections; the closes that fit them; riskier to say no; childhood references; layers of protection; hours for objections; "only costs a call" in the CTA; the cost of inaction shown; the guarantee named.

## Examples

## Mistakes he calls out
- Selling only the gain: "Focus on the cost of inaction, not just the benefit of action." [doc L16877]
- A guarantee chosen without the refund math, above all when inflation rises. [doc L16659]
- Leaving them on the fence: "that's less important to me than you dying on the fence." [doc L16801]

## Lines worth keeping
- "Show how it's riskier to say NO than YES - and be willing to spend HOURS handling objections. That's where all the money is." [doc L16623]

- "Winners do it - so you're encouraging people to do the thing that winners will do. But most people aren't ready to win yet." [doc L16699]

## After Harvest

**From the doc** (for the Black Friday offer, $0 upfront plus 15% of revenue)
- Guarantee: conditional better than money back. The 30-day standard, plus double the money back if they follow the blueprint, send our emails and don't make 2x what they paid in 60 days; condition: the Google Doc log. [doc L16809-16835]
- "I can do this myself": "The question isn't CAN you do it - it's WILL you do it". [doc L16841-16843]
- "My list is too small": "the smaller your list, the MORE important it is that you maximize every dollar per subscriber." [doc L16845-16847]
- "I don't want to over-email": "The risk isn't over-emailing. The risk is under-serving." [doc L16849-16851]
- "15% seems high": "So is 85% of something better than 100% of nothing?" [doc L16853-16855]
- More time on objections than on the pitch; show the cost of another failed Black Friday. [doc L16883-16887]

**Our reading** (ours)
- Two uses: our own pitch, and the guarantee and objection section of each client's event offer.
- The Google Doc condition suits a buyer doing the work; for a done-for-you build the condition must be something the client does.
